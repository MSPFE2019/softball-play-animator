# Softball Play Animator

Interactive, animated softball play designer for youth coaches and defensive teaching. Supports 9- or 10-player defenses, adjustable infield depth, draggable fielders, custom play editing, runner-path adjustments, and voice-guided explanations tailored for 10U-12U learning.

**Live:** https://mspfe2019.github.io/softball-play-animator/

Single self-contained `index.html` - no build, no dependencies.

Features:
- defensive-focused play library with batting and bunting reads
- voice narration for the coaching cue and what players need to do
- alignment presets for standard, shifted, 10U, and 12U teaching models
- custom play creation and save/export for coach cards
- read-react-throw teaching loop with pre-pitch assignments, bunt communication, and backup responsibilities

## Coaching principles

The built-in strategy guide is shaped around age-appropriate defensive habits:

- prepare before the pitch, read the ball, then make the safest useful throw
- communicate assignments with short calls such as “Mine,” “Two,” and “One”
- teach bunt defense as coordinated coverage: corners charge, the pitcher covers the middle, and middle infielders cover bases
- practice situations progressively: show the movement, do it together, then add live reads and game speed
- use game-day preparation, warmups, and team communication to build confidence before the first pitch
